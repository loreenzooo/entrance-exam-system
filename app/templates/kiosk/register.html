Biometric Consent Notice

Before proceeding, please read and acknowledge how your biometric data will be used.

Purpose of Collection

This system captures your facial image to generate a facial embedding, a numerical representation of your facial features. This embedding will be used to assist in identity verification and attendance monitoring during the entrance examination.

Data Storage and Privacy

Your facial embedding will be processed and stored for the system's demonstration and attendance verification purposes. Access to the data should be restricted to authorized personnel, and the data should be protected against unauthorized access or misuse.

Data Retention

Biometric data will be retained only for the specified demonstration period or as otherwise explained in the system's privacy notice, after which it will be securely deleted, subject to applicable requirements.
